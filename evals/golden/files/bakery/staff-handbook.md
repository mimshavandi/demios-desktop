# Harrow & Pike Bakery: staff handbook
Version 4, January 2026. Manager: Mia Harrow. Owner: Theo Pike.

## 1. Opening hours
- Tuesday to Friday: 07:00–18:00
- Saturday: 07:00–13:00
- Sunday: 08:00–12:00
- Monday: closed (deep clean and ordering day for the baking team)

## 2. Shifts and breaks
- Bakers start at 04:00. Counter staff start 30 minutes before opening.
- Shifts are published every Thursday for the following week in the rota book by the till.
- Shift swaps: agree them with a colleague and write them in the rota book at least 48 hours before the shift. Mia must initial the swap.
- Breaks: a shift over 6 hours includes a 30-minute unpaid break. A shift over 9 hours includes a further 15-minute paid break.

## 3. Sickness and absence
- If you are ill, phone Mia on 555-0142 by 06:00 (bakers: by 03:30). Do not text or message; phone.
- After three days of absence in a row, bring a note from your doctor.
- Anyone with vomiting or diarrhoea must not work with food until 48 hours after symptoms stop.

## 4. Holidays
- 25 days of paid holiday a year plus public holidays, pro rata for part-time staff.
- Request holiday at least four weeks ahead in the holiday book. No holiday in the two weeks before Christmas.

## 5. Food safety
- Wash hands on entering the kitchen, after breaks and after handling raw eggs.
- Hair nets in the kitchen at all times. No jewellery except a plain band ring.
- Fridge temperatures (5 °C or below) are recorded twice a day, at opening and at 14:00.

## 6. Allergens
- Every product's allergens are on the price sheet and the counter cards.
- We use nuts, sesame, milk, egg and soya in the same kitchen. We **cannot** promise any product is free from traces. Say this to every customer who asks about an allergy, then show them the allergen card.
- Never guess. If you are unsure, ask the baker on shift.

## 7. Cash and till
- Count the float (€150) at opening and closing.
- A difference of more than €5 must be reported to Mia the same day and written in the till log.

## 8. Staff perks
- Staff discount: 30% on all items for yourself and your household.
- Day-old bread is free for staff at closing time.

## 9. Uniform
Black trousers or skirt, the bakery apron (provided) and closed, non-slip shoes.

# Seeded rye sourdough
Makes one 1 kg loaf (use a 1 kg loaf tin, greased).

## Ingredients
- 150 g active rye sourdough starter
- 500 g rye flour (type 1150)
- 400 ml lukewarm water
- 12 g salt
- 100 g mixed seeds (sunflower and pumpkin), plus extra for the top

## Method
1. Mix everything into a sticky paste. Rye dough is not kneaded.
2. Spread into the tin, smooth the top with a wet spoon and sprinkle seeds on top.
3. Cover and leave somewhere warm for 3–4 hours, until it has risen by about a third and cracks appear.
4. Bake at **230 °C for 15 minutes**, then turn down to **200 °C for 45 minutes**.
5. Turn out and cool on a rack for at least 12 hours before slicing.

# Chickpea and spinach curry with cashew sauce
Serves 3. Takes 30 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- a thumb of fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400 g)
- 2 tins chickpeas (400 g each), drained
- 400 ml tin coconut milk
- 50 g cashew nuts, soaked in hot water for 10 minutes and blended to a paste
- 200 g fresh spinach
- Salt; rice or flatbread to serve

## Method
1. Fry the onion in the oil for 6 minutes, then add garlic, ginger and spices for 1 minute.
2. Add tomatoes, chickpeas and coconut milk. Simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Stir in the cashew paste and the spinach until the spinach wilts. Season.

# Red lentil soup
Serves 4. Takes 35 minutes. Vegan. Freezes well for up to 3 months.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 250 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400 g)
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock
- Juice of half a lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan. Cook the onion and carrots gently for 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils are soft.
4. Blend until smooth, or leave chunky. Stir in the lemon juice and season.

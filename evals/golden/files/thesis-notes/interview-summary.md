# Interview summary (12 interviews, February–March 2026)

Recruited through the scheme mailing list and the noticeboard at the Lindenfeld library. Interviews lasted 35 to 70 minutes (average 48). All recorded with consent and transcribed.

## Participants
- Still composting: 7 (P01, P02, P04, P06, P09, P11, P12)
- Stopped: 5 (P03, P05, P07, P08, P10)

## Codes (number of participants mentioning it)
| Code | All 12 | Of the 5 who stopped |
|---|---|---|
| Convenience / distance to bin | 9 | 5 |
| Smell or pests (wasps, rats) | 6 | 3 |
| Social contact at the bin | 5 | 0 |
| Forgetting / no routine | 4 | 3 |
| Composting at home instead | 3 | 2 |

## Notes
- Every participant who stopped mentioned distance or convenience.
- "Social contact" came up only among people still composting: chatting with neighbours at the bin.
- P08 and P10 now compost in their own gardens, so they haven't really stopped composting, only stopped using the scheme.

## Quotes
- P05: "In summer the bin is fine, but in November it's dark at five and it's a ten-minute walk. I just stopped."
- P11: "Honestly I go partly to see who's around. It's become a little chat spot."
- P07: "The wasps in August finished it for me."

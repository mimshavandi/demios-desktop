# Notes from Plot 9 (blog by Hal)

## The slugs won again

Friend, I lost the lettuces. All of them. I went out on Tuesday with my torch and counted forty-one slugs on one row. I did not count the rest. Some battles you lose with dignity, and some you just lose.

So this year I am trying copper tape. My neighbour Ruth says it is nonsense. Ruth is usually right. We will see.

— Hal

## On the matter of rhubarb

Friend, rhubarb asks for nothing. You put it in a corner, you forget it, and in April it waves at you like an old dog that never stopped waiting. I pulled the first stalks this morning. Too sour to eat raw, which did not stop me.

My one piece of advice: don't harvest in the first year. Let it settle in. It is a slow friendship.

— Hal

## Rain, finally

Friend, it rained. Three days of it. The water butts are full and I have been sitting in the shed with a flask, watching the beans drink. There are worse ways to spend a Thursday. I can't think of many better ones.

— Hal

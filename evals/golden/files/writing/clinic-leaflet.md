BROOKMERE FAMILY PRACTICE - PATIENT INFORMATION

Our practice, which is located at 12 Mill Row, Brookmere, is open to patients on weekdays, that is to say Monday through to Friday, between the hours of 8:00 in the morning and 18:30 in the evening, and in addition to this we are also open on Saturday mornings from 9:00 until 12:00, although please note that on Saturdays only pre-booked appointments can be seen and there is no walk-in service available at that time.

Should you require a repeat prescription, it is necessary for requests to be made either through the online form at https://brookmere-practice.example.org/repeat or alternatively by handing in the paper slip at reception, and you should be aware that it will take two full working days before your prescription is ready to collect, so please plan ahead accordingly and do not leave it until the last minute.

For appointments, please telephone us on 555-0142. Lines tend to be at their busiest between 8:00 and 9:30, so if your matter is not urgent we would ask that you kindly call later in the day.

This year's flu vaccination clinic will be held on Wednesday 14 October 2026. Patients aged 65 and over, and patients with certain long-term conditions, are eligible, and it is required that you book a slot in advance.

If you need urgent medical help when the practice is closed, call the out-of-hours service on 555-0199.

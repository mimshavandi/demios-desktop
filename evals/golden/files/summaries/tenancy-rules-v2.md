# Birkenhof Housing Co-op: house rules (version 2, adopted at the members' meeting, 9 September 2026)

1. **Quiet hours.** 22:00–07:00 Sunday to Thursday nights; 23:00–08:00 on Friday and Saturday nights. No loud music, drilling or parties in these hours.
2. **Bicycles.** Bicycles are kept in the new bike shed in the courtyard. The cellar bike room is now a storage room and bikes may no longer be kept there. Not in the stairwell or hallways.
3. **Pets.** Cats and small animals are allowed, up to two pets per household. Dogs need written approval from the co-op board.
4. **Laundry room.** Book a slot on the tablet by the laundry room door. Maximum two slots per household per week.
5. **Balconies.** Only electric barbecues are allowed on balconies. Charcoal and gas barbecues may be used at the grill area in the courtyard. Laundry may be dried on balconies.
6. **Guests.** Guests may stay up to 4 weeks in a row. Longer stays need board approval.
7. **Keys.** Replacement keys cost a €50 deposit.
8. **Rubbish.** Separate paper, glass, packaging and food waste. Bulky waste only on the collection day announced on the noticeboard.
9. **Shared garden.** Open to all residents from 08:00 to 22:00.

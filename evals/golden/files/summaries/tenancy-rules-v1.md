# Birkenhof Housing Co-op: house rules (version 1, March 2024)

1. **Quiet hours.** 22:00–07:00 every day. No loud music, drilling or parties in these hours.
2. **Bicycles.** Bicycles are kept in the cellar bike room. Not in the stairwell or hallways.
3. **Pets.** Cats and small animals are allowed. Dogs need written approval from the co-op board.
4. **Laundry room.** Book a slot on the paper sheet on the laundry room door. Maximum two slots per household per week.
5. **Balconies.** Charcoal and gas barbecues are allowed on balconies. Laundry may not be dried on balconies where it can be seen from the street.
6. **Guests.** Guests may stay up to 4 weeks in a row. Longer stays need board approval.
7. **Keys.** Replacement keys cost a €30 deposit.
8. **Rubbish.** Separate paper, glass, packaging and food waste. Bulky waste only on the collection day announced on the noticeboard.
9. **Shared garden.** Open to all residents from 08:00 to 22:00.

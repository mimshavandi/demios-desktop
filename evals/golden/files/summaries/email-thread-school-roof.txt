Hello Sven, Joanna,

Thanks for logging this promptly. Roof replacements are exactly what the schools capital fund is for. The rules for this round:

- The fund pays 70% of the cost of the work, and the school pays 30% from its own budget or reserves.
- It only funds work that fixes the problem properly. A partial repair with a five-year life would not normally qualify; a full replacement would.
- Applications for this round must reach us by 30 November 2026. Decisions are made in January.
- Work must be done in school holidays.

I'd need the contractor's quote, the survey, photos and a governors' approval of the school's 30% share.

Best wishes,
Lionel Fairweather
Property Officer, Haldorf Council

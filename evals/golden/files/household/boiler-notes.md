# Boiler notes (gas combi boiler in the utility room)

- Installed: 2019 by Kessler Heating
- Last service: 14 January 2026. Next service due: January 2027 (yearly).
- Kessler Heating: 555-0155, office@kessler-heating.test. Ask for Paula.

## Pressure
The gauge on the front should read between **1.0 and 1.5 bar** when the heating is off.

**If it is below 0.8 bar:**
1. Turn the boiler off.
2. Underneath the boiler there are two small taps on the silver filling loop.
3. Open both taps slowly until the gauge reads 1.2 bar, then close them both firmly.
4. Turn the boiler back on.

If the pressure drops below 0.8 bar again within a week, there may be a leak: call Kessler Heating rather than topping up again.

**If it is above 2.5 bar**, turn the boiler off and call Kessler Heating.

## Reset
If the red light flashes, press and hold the reset button for 5 seconds. Try only twice. If it still flashes, call Kessler Heating.

## Smell of gas
Do not use the boiler or any switches. Open windows, leave the house and call the gas emergency line.

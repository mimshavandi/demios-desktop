# Brightwell WM-714 washing machine: quick guide (excerpt)

## Error codes
| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | No water coming in | Check the tap behind the machine is open and the inlet hose isn't kinked. |
| E3 | Water not draining | Clean the drain filter (see below). Check the drain hose isn't kinked. |
| E5 | Door lock fault | Close the door firmly and restart. If it repeats, call service. |
| E7 | Unbalanced load | Spread the laundry out or remove a heavy item, then restart the spin. |

## Cleaning the drain filter
1. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. Open the small flap at the bottom right of the front.
3. Put a shallow tray and a towel underneath; some water will come out.
4. Turn the filter anticlockwise slowly and pull it out.
5. Rinse it under the tap and remove fluff, coins or hair clips.
6. Screw it back in clockwise until it is tight, close the flap, plug in.
Clean the filter every three months, or when E3 shows.

## Programmes
- Cottons 40 °C: 2 h 50 min
- Eco 40-60: 3 h 20 min (lowest energy)
- Quick 30: 30 min, max 3 kg
- Wool / hand wash: 50 min

# Home contents insurance: policy summary

**Insurer:** Ostwald Mutual
**Policy number:** HC-55-0192
**Policyholders:** Edith and Karl Brenner, 7 Weidenweg, Lindenfeld
**Period:** 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027 (renews 1 March 2027)
**Monthly cost:** €18.40 a month by direct debit
**Claims line:** 555-0177 (24 hours), or claims@ostwald-mutual.test

## What is covered
- Contents of the home up to **€45,000** against fire, storm, flood, burst pipes, theft and attempted theft.
- Valuables (jewellery, watches, cameras) up to €1,500 per item and €6,000 in total.
- **Bicycles at home:** covered as contents when kept in a locked house, garage or shed.
- **Bicycles away from home:** up to €1,000 per bike, only if locked to a fixed object. Theft between 22:00 and 06:00 is covered only if the bike was in a locked room.
- Freezer contents spoiled by a power cut: up to €400.

## What is not covered
- **Accidental damage** (for example spills on carpets or sofas, dropped TVs). This is an optional add-on; it was offered at renewal and **not taken**.
- Wear and tear, damp, moths and vermin.
- Theft by someone you let into the home.
- Contents of a home left empty for more than 60 days in a row.

## Excess
You pay the first **€250** of every claim (€500 for flood claims).

## How to claim
1. For theft or attempted theft, report it to the police first and note the reference number.
2. Phone the claims line or email within **30 days** of the loss.
3. Keep receipts, photos or bank statements that show what the item was worth.

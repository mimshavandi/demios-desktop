---
name: critique
description: Gives honest, specific feedback on a piece of writing or a design the user shares, with concrete fixes.
allowed-tools: []
license: Apache-2.0
---
Review what the user shares and help them improve it.

- Start with what works, in one or two sentences, so they know what to keep.
- Then list the main problems, most important first. For each: what the problem is, where it is (quote a few words or name the part), and a concrete fix.
- Judge it against its goal and audience. If you don't know them, say what you assumed.
- For writing, look at clarity, structure, tone and length. For a design, look at hierarchy, readability, consistency and whether the main action is obvious.
- Limit yourself to the five most useful points. Don't rewrite the whole thing unless the user asks.

# Connect (draft section for the privacy policy)

Tool servers are in Settings → Tools. Connect lists tool servers run by the services themselves, for example Linear or Notion. When you select Connect, Demios adds that service's server like any other remote tool server: you sign in on the service's own page, the sign-in is saved in your system keychain, and what you send goes from your device straight to that service. Demios doesn't receive it. That service's own privacy policy applies to what you send it. You can remove a tool server at any time in Settings → Tools, which also deletes its saved sign-in from your keychain. The list ships with the app and is checked against Demios's signature; choosing from it sends nothing to Demios.
